## 📱 VARIANTE 1: "Problem-Benefit" - El Cansancio de las 3pm

### Especificaciones Técnicas
- **Nombre**: Problem-Benefit
- **Objetivo**: Identificar + Resolver rápido
- **Formato**: 1:1 (cuadrado para Instagram Feed)
- **Resolución**: 1080x1080px (también disponible 2K para futuro)
- **Modelo Recomendado**: marketing_studio_image (Higgsfield)

### Prompt Optimizado para Generación

```
Professional man (45 years old) in modern home office, checking wristwatch showing 3:00 PM, 
tired exhausted expression, rubbing eyes, business casual clothing. Warm golden afternoon 
window light from left side, soft shadows, natural daylight. 

Subject positioned left 60%, white background right 40% for ad copy. 
Warm color grading, approachable, authentic fatigue, relatable moment. 
No harsh shadows, soft dimensional lighting. High-key bright feel. 

Camera: 50mm f/1.8, ISO 200, warm 4800K color temperature.
Comparable to wellness brand photography.

Format: 1080x1080px square. High-res JPG.
```

### Prompts Alternativos (A/B Testing)

**Variante A - Female Focus:**
```
Professional woman (40 years old) at office desk, 3pm visible on clock/watch, 
visibly tired, resting head on hand, exhausted but determined expression. 
Natural window light, warm tones, same composition (subject left 60%).
```

**Variante B - More Dramatic Fatigue:**
```
Professional person (35-50 years) staring at desk calendar/clock showing 3pm, 
extreme tiredness evident in face, slumped posture, but not depressed. 
Golden afternoon light, warm aesthetic, contemplative moment.
```

## 📸 VARIANTE 2: "Social-Proof" - María Transformada (Testimonial)

### Especificaciones Técnicas
- **Nombre**: Social-Proof-Testimonial
- **Objetivo**: Generar confianza + FOMO
- **Formato**: 4:5 (vertical para Instagram Reels/Stories)
- **Resolución**: 1080x1350px (4:5 aspect ratio optimal)
- **Modelo Recomendado**: marketing_studio_image (Higgsfield) o soul_2 (para retratos)

### Prompt Optimizado para Generación

```
Professional woman (42 years old, Latina, Portuguese style) smiling genuinely 
in bright modern café, natural window light from left, warm and energetic expression, 
confident posture. Wearing blazer, professional but relaxed. 

Green plants visible in soft bokeh background, warm gold accents from sunlight. 
Natural fresh look, no overdone makeup. Hands visible, natural position. 
Eyes engaged, authentic joy visible.

Warm color grading, vibrant energy, authentic joy, transformed energy visible. 
Comparable to testimonial DTC brand photography, aspirational yet relatable.

Camera: 85mm f/1.4, ISO 200-400, f/1.8 aperture, warm 5200K.
Format: 1080x1350px (4:5). High-res JPG.
```

### Prompts Alternativos (A/B Testing)

**Variante A - At Home Office:**
```
Same woman at modern home office/workspace, laptop visible, confident smile, 
hands on desk or keyboard, warm natural light, green plants background, 
professional casual clothing, energetic presence.
```

**Variante B - Coffee Shop Close-up:**
```
Woman (42) holding coffee cup, smiling at camera, cozy café ambiance, 
warm sunlight through window creating rim light on hair, intimate portrait 
with product visible subtly in background.
```

**Variante C - Standing by Window:**
```
Full-body shot, woman standing by bright window, looking out with confident smile, 
relaxed posture, modern interior background, natural golden light, 
professional lifestyle aesthetic.
```

## 🧬 VARIANTE 3: "Science-Curiosity" - Energía Celular Activada

### Especificaciones Técnicas
- **Nombre**: Science-Curiosity
- **Objetivo**: Educación + Intriga
- **Formato**: 1:1 (cuadrado para Instagram Feed)
- **Resolución**: 1080x1080px (también 2K para futuro)
- **Modelo Recomendado**: marketing_studio_image (Higgsfield) - estilo digital/abstract

### Prompt Optimizado para Generación

```
Abstract scientific visualization: glowing molecular structure with green (#10B981) 
nucleus core emitting light, blue (#3B82F6) orbiting electrons, 
gold (#F59E0B) energy rays emanating outward. 

Floating particles catching light. Dark charcoal blue background. 
Volumetric light effects, subtle glow, pulsing energy effect suggesting activation. 

Modern digital 3D render style, sophisticated scientific aesthetic. 
Vibrant, energetic, dynamic, not static. 

Colors: 40% green, 30% blue, 20% gold, 10% dark background. 
Modern wellness meets biotech branding.

Format: 1080x1080px square. High-res JPG.
```

### Prompts Alternativos (A/B Testing)

**Variante A - More Particles:**
```
Molecular energy visualization with MORE floating micro-particles (bokeh-like), 
creating sense of movement and life. Green nucleus pulsing with blue and gold accents. 
Deep space aesthetic. Dynamic, not static.
```

**Variante B - Simplified/Minimalist:**
```
Single glowing molecule center-frame, green core, blue electrons in orbit, 
gold light rays. Minimal floating particles. Very clean, sophisticated, 
high-tech aesthetic.
```

**Variante C - DNA Helix Variant:**
```
DNA helix structure with energy flowing through it (green + blue + gold), 
cells/particles activating along the spiral. Scientific but modern, 
representing natural energy activation.
```

## 🎯 Instrucciones de Uso

### Para Generar en Higgsfield:
1. Ir a Higgsfield
2. Seleccionar modelo: `marketing_studio_image`
3. Copiar prompt exacto de arriba
4. Seleccionar aspect ratio (1:1 para variante 1 y 3, 4:5 para variante 2)
5. Resolución: 2K (para máxima calidad)
6. Generar

### Para Contratar Fotógrafo Profesional:
1. Compartir el prompt técnico completo
2. Mencionar las especificaciones de cámara (lentes, apertura, ISO)
3. Compartir referencias de color grading (warm, vibrant, scientific)
4. Proporcionar brief de composición (60/40 split para var 1, centered para var 3)
