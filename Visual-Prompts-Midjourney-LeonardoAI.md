## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Midjourney loves specificity**: Camera specs and lens details work great
2. **Leonardo AI prefers mood/style**: Focus on emotion and aesthetic references
3. **Both respond to "professional/editorial"**: Universal quality signal
4. **Color temperature in Kelvin works**: "3200K warm", "5500K daylight"
5. **Comparative aesthetics help**: "Etsy brand", "editorial lifestyle", "ASMR aesthetic"
6. **Avoid conflicting instructions**: Don't ask for "soft light" AND "dramatic shadows"
7. **Test aspect ratios**: Same prompt different AR may surprise you
8. **Warm tones = conversions**: Both platforms render warm colors better for sales
9. **Hands visible = higher engagement**: For product/process content, hands matter
10. **Meditative mood = watches video**: "slow", "meditative", "hypnotic" = watch time
