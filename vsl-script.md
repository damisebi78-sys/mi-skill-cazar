## 🎬 VISUAL DIRECTION

### Style Guide
- **Aesthetic**: Modern, clean, hopeful
- **Color Palette**: Verde (#10B981) + Azul (#3B82F6) + Blanco
- **Tone**: Professional but warm, not corporate
- **Pacing**: Slow-medium (not hyper-cuts)
- **Music**: Ambient, uplifting (90-100 BPM)

### Shot List
```
0-3: Reloj/frustración (1 shot, 3 seg)
3-15: Montaje problemático (4-5 shots, 12 seg)
15-30: Producto + animación (3-4 shots, 15 seg)
30-45: Testimonios video (2-3 testimonios, 15 seg)
45-60: Landing page + CTA (2-3 shots, 15 seg)
```

### Music & Sound Design
- **Background Music**: Emotional, building (royalty-free)
- **Transitions**: Soft fades (no jarring cuts)
- **Narrator Voice**: Professional, male/female (test both)
- **SFX**: Minimal (alert sound on hook, success chime on CTA)

---

## 🎥 PRODUCTION SPECS

### Video Format
- **Resolution**: 1080p (Full HD) + 4K option
- **Aspect Ratio**: 
  - 16:9 (desktop/YouTube)
  - 9:16 (mobile/stories)
  - 1:1 (feed/reels)
- **File Format**: MP4 (H.264, AAC audio)
- **Frame Rate**: 24fps (cinematic)

### File Sizes
```
16:9 (YouTube): ~100-150MB (4K), ~30-50MB (1080p)
9:16 (Mobile): ~50-80MB (4K), ~15-25MB (1080p)
1:1 (Feed): ~40-60MB (4K), ~12-20MB (1080p)
```

### Audio Specs
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit
- **Format**: Stereo
- **Loudness**: -16 LUFS (YouTube standard)

## 🎙️ NARRATOR OPTIONS

### Option A: Professional Male (Authority)
- **Voice**: Deep, warm, trustworthy
- **Pace**: Measured, not rushed
- **Accent**: Neutral European (English or dubbed)
- **Use**: Main hook + solution + CTA

### Option B: Professional Female (Empathy)
- **Voice**: Warm, understanding, hopeful
- **Pace**: Natural, conversational
- **Accent**: Neutral European
- **Use**: Problem section + transition

### Recommendation: Mix Both
- **Hook + Problem**: Female (empathy)
- **Solution + CTA**: Male (authority)
- **Total Narrator Time**: ~45 seconds
